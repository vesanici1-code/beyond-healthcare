Hair restoration is not simply about moving follicles. It begins with understanding your hair loss, donor area, desired hairline and the result that is realistic for you.


Through BEYOND, patients can be connected with Turkish hair-transplant expertise in Kosovo, creating a more convenient pathway to specialised hair restoration without having to organise every part of the journey alone.

THE METHOD

FUE
HAIR
TRANSPLANT

Follicular Unit Extraction, commonly known as FUE, involves harvesting individual follicular units from the donor area and placing them into areas where hair restoration is needed.

01

Individual Follicles

Follicular units are individually extracted from the selected donor area rather than removing a strip of scalp.

02

Natural Planning

The treatment plan considers the hairline, density, donor area and the patient's individual restoration goals.

03

Personalised Treatment

Your treatment pathway is determined after an individual assessment rather than using the same approach for every patient.
