Choose a treatment area below. Our team can help you understand the next step and connect your enquiry with the appropriate clinical team.

01 · BREAST

Breast procedures

Enhancement, lifting and reduction options designed around your proportions, anatomy and individual goals.

01

Breast Enhancement

Breast augmentation increases volume and can improve shape using implants or, when suitable, fat transfer.

02

Breast Lift

A breast lift reshapes and elevates breast tissue to address sagging and create a more lifted contour.

03

Breast Reduction

Selected breast tissue is removed and the breasts are reshaped to create a smaller, more proportionate contour.

02 · BODY

Body contouring

Popular procedures for reshaping areas affected by excess fat, loose skin, weight change or pregnancy.

01

Abdominoplasty + Liposuction

A tummy tuck removes selected excess abdominal skin and may tighten the abdominal wall; liposuction can refine surrounding contours.

02

Liposuction

Localized fat deposits can be removed from areas such as the abdomen, waist, thighs, arms, back or chin to refine contours.

03

Thigh Lift

A thigh lift removes selected excess skin and reshapes the thigh area when skin laxity is the main concern.

04

Arm Lift

An arm lift reshapes the upper arm by removing selected excess skin and, where appropriate, combining it with contouring.

05

Mommy Makeover

A personalised combination of procedures, often involving the breasts and abdomen, designed around changes following pregnancy and weight fluctuations.

06

Lipofilling

Fat is collected from another area, processed and transferred to add volume or improve contour in selected areas such as the breasts, face or body.

03 · FACE

Face & profile

Refine, refresh or rebalance facial features with a treatment plan tailored to your anatomy and goals.

01

Face Lift

A facelift repositions and tightens selected facial tissues and removes excess skin to improve facial contour and visible signs of ageing.

02

Rhinoplasty

Rhinoplasty reshapes the nose to improve proportion and, when needed, address functional concerns. The plan is highly individual.

03

Blepharoplasty

Eyelid surgery removes or repositions selected excess skin and tissue around the upper and/or lower eyelids for a more refreshed appearance.

04

Neck Lift

A neck lift addresses loose skin and selected neck tissues to create a cleaner transition between the jawline and neck.
